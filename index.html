03 Custom Website · PHP · SEO

VakuumLift

A business website for a company that rents and sells vacuum glass lifters — equipment that lets a single operator safely place large glass panes and panels with a crane. I built it from scratch, without a CMS, in HTML, CSS, PHP and JavaScript, so it loads fast and every page is tailored to what contractors search for. Clear product pages, a price list and visible "Request a quote" buttons turn visitors into rental and sales inquiries.

  • Rental (daily, weekly, monthly) & sales pages
  • Quote request & contact form
  • SEO for local searches (e.g. “iznajmljivanje vakuumskog podizača”)

About Me

I'm a full-stack web developer from Belgrade who loves turning ideas into products people actually enjoy using.
